FAQ

Everything you need to know before your appointment.

How do I book an appointment?

Choose your service, select your preferred stylist and time, and complete the booking form. You’ll get a confirmation after submission.

Do you groom all breeds and sizes?

Yes. Our team handles small dogs, large breeds, puppies, senior pets, and mixed breeds with individualized care plans.

Is there a cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 24 hours notice for reschedules or cancellations. This helps us keep time available for other pets.

What should I bring for my pet?

Bring your pet’s favorite treat, any current medical notes, and anything you want us to know about sensitivities or anxiety triggers.

How long does a grooming visit take?

Most appointments take about 60 minutes, depending on coat condition, breed, and service type.

Still have a question?

Our team is happy to help you pick the right service and schedule.
